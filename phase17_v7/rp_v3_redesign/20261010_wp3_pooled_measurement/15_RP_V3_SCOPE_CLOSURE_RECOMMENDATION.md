# RP v3 scope-closure recommendation

**Date:** 2026-10-10

**Technical recommendation:** `CLOSE_CURRENT_RP_V3_COMPUTATIONAL_SCOPE`

## What RP v3 established

1. WP1 showed that the primary discovery effect was robust to the frozen A1/A2
   representation sensitivities on common support, without establishing
   annotation independence or equivalence.
2. WP2/G2 and the single permitted G2B interval amendment did not satisfy the
   full prespecified operating-characteristic conjunction. The WP2 method branch
   is therefore permanently closed rather than eligible for another rescue.
3. WP3 verified the external sufficient-count objects and added a bounded pooled
   measurement result: `T_frozen` and `T_rank` preserve donor ordering closely,
   and no one frozen IFN gene dominates the ordering.
4. RP v3 did not unlock corrected external disease outcomes, a clinical score,
   source-label-independent transfer, causal regulatory evidence or manuscript
   replacement.

## Recommended author decision

Accept the current RP v3 record as a completed methodological and measurement
audit, while retaining the historical manuscript's bounded positive conclusion:
reproducible process-level B_CONV IFN/ISG remodeling within explicit identity,
transfer and mechanistic limits.

Do not initiate another interval method, mapper, transform, gene-set search,
external disease model or post-hoc rescue. A new analysis branch should require
genuinely new data or a new research question with a separately approved plan.

## Permitted next actions after author closure

- archive the canonical RP v3 decision record and keep all HOLDs visible;
- decide whether the new pooled measurement fact belongs only in internal
  provenance or warrants a narrowly bounded manuscript/supplement statement;
- if manuscript integration is requested, open a separate claim-ownership and
  text/figure impact review before modifying any canonical manuscript object;
- keep journal submission, GitHub Release and Zenodo changes on HOLD until that
  author-level scientific decision is recorded.

- WP4 analysis;
- automatic manuscript, figure, submission-package, Release or Zenodo changes;
- claims of external validation, clinical utility, platform equivalence or
