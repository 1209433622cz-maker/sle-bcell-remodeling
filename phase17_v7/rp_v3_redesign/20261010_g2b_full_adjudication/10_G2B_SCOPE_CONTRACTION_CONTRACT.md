## 2. WP3 is not automatically unlocked

RP v3 defines WP3 as a separable external frozen-score/association question.
The G2B HOLD permits only a result-blind WP3 go/no-go preflight. It does not
authorize immediate external effect estimation.

Before any WP3 outcome is examined, a compact protocol must freeze:

1. the scientific question and the knowledge gain required beyond the already
   established positive IFN/ISG replication;
2. the external cohort, biological unit, repeated-sample handling and complete
   eligibility accounting;
3. `T_frozen` as the primary score, including exact feature list, background,
   transformation, direction and missing-feature policy;
4. `T_rank` as a sensitivity only, with no selection based on the smaller
   external P value;
5. the role of `T_local` and the prohibition on presenting a refitted local
   score as independent validation;
6. reference/external normalization and calibration gates, with fail-closed
   handling of mapping or coverage defects;
7. estimands, covariates, test families, multiplicity control, uncertainty
   intervals and stopping rules;
8. exact input hashes, software environment and an effect-free dry run.

A third cohort may be considered only if a prespecified scientific gap and an
eligible source are documented before results. It may not be added to rescue a
negative WP3 result.

## 3. WP3 go/no-go rule

WP3 may proceed only if the preflight demonstrates that the frozen analysis can
add information not already owned by the historical external IFN replication.
Examples of non-incremental work include simply showing another positive IFN
score, selecting whichever score gives the smallest P value, or relabeling an
uncalibrated transfer result as validation.

If incremental knowledge, eligible support or fail-closed calibration cannot be
specified prospectively, the decision is `NO_GO_WP3__RETROGRADE_TO_RP_V3_SCOPE_CLOSE`.
The RP v3 redesign should then be retained as a bounded methodological record or
retopiced rather than forced into the current manuscript.

## 4. WP4 and publication boundary

WP4 remains closed. It can be reconsidered only after a separately frozen WP3
passes and demonstrates interpretive value beyond the existing manuscript.

Until then, do not:

- rewrite the manuscript around RP v3 results;
- replace current figures or source data;
- rebuild a journal submission package;
- create a GitHub release or Zenodo version for RP v3;
- state that WP2, WP3 or WP4 has validated a disease mechanism.
