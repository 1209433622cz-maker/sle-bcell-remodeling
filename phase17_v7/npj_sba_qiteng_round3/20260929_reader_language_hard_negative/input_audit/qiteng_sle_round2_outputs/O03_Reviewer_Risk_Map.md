# O03 Reviewer Risk Map

## HIGH — novelty may be misread as “IFN in SLE is already known”

**Risk:** A reviewer could reduce the manuscript to another report of an interferon signature in SLE B cells.
**Why this matters:** the biological pathway itself is not novel enough to carry the paper.
**Current mitigation:** Title, Abstract, Results and Discussion frame the contribution as **layer-specific reproducibility under identity/transfer stress tests**, not rediscovery of interferon biology. The revised cover letter now makes this the editorial-level advance.
**Do not do:** inflate novelty by claiming a new interferon mechanism or new taxonomy.

## HIGH — external replication is source-label-defined

**Risk:** A reviewer may argue that the external signal depends on source annotation.
**Current mitigation:** this limitation is not hidden. The corrected label-independent mapper is explicitly tested and fails the required B_ASC precision criterion; no corrected disease effect is estimated.
**Correct answer:** external evidence supports a prespecified process-level IFN program within a source-label-defined broad-B analogue, not transferable de novo taxonomy.

## HIGH — regulator inference could be viewed as circular or mechanistic overreach

**Risk:** CollecTRI activity uses disease-ranked statistics and overlaps interferon biology.
**Current mitigation:** cross-method CAMERA/FRY sensitivity, leave-one-target/target resampling and two depletion branches are retained; broader M5911 depletion weakens discovery STAT2.
**Claim ceiling:** “IFN-centred regulatory context” / “observational convergence”, never “STAT1/STAT2-driven causal mechanism”.

## MEDIUM-HIGH — broad two-compartment scaffold may appear overly coarse

**Risk:** readers accustomed to fine B-cell taxonomies may see B_CONV/B_ASC as biologically reductive.
**Mitigation:** fine states are not silently discarded; the fine solution fails prespecified stability criteria and is retained as a negative result. The broad partition is explicitly an analysis scaffold.

## MEDIUM — timing of overlap-depletion sensitivity

**Risk:** depletion was added after the core regulator analysis was fixed.
**Mitigation:** the Methods now say exactly that in reader-facing language and preserve separate six-test sensitivity families. The original 24-test regulator family is not replaced.

## MEDIUM — incomplete clinical covariates

**Risk:** treatment, sex and detailed clinical covariates are not consistently harmonized across public cohorts.
**Mitigation:** limitation remains explicit; no clinical prediction/utility claim is made.

## MEDIUM — small adult external stratum and n=2 perturbation context

**Risk:** adult-only external estimate is imprecise; GSE23307 has two healthy donors.
**Mitigation:** adult result is directional only; GSE23307 is descriptive response context with no inferential P value.

## MEDIUM — complex multiplicity structure

**Risk:** multiple program, gene-level, regulator and sensitivity families could be misunderstood.
**Mitigation:** Methods state family ownership; Supplementary Data retain full statistical results; the revised text distinguishes the original 24-test family from later six-test depletion families.

## LOW-MEDIUM — figure terminology retains internal “frozen” labels

**Risk:** Figure 2c / Figure 3 contain reader-visible “Frozen” terminology that is technically meaningful internally but less transparent than `Primary` / `Prespecified`.
**Next action:** source-level text-only rebuild, not PDF annotation or data replotting.

## POLICY/INTERFACE — AI disclosure

**Risk:** inconsistent disclosure across files could trigger editorial questions.
**Mitigation:** manuscript, cover letter, declarations and reporting information now consistently state the assistance scope, local execution against fixed inputs, author-led interpretation/final decisions and author accountability.
