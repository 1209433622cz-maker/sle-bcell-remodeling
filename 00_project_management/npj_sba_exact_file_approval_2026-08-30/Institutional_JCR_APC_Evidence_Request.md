## Evidence interpretation rule

Springer Nature's public Hong Kong agreement pages list "The Chinese University
of Hong Kong" and describe corresponding-author eligibility, but they do not
establish that CUHK-Shenzhen is the same eligible institution or that this exact
fully OA journal and Article are covered. Public agreement text is supporting
context only. A dated institutional or publisher verification is required.
