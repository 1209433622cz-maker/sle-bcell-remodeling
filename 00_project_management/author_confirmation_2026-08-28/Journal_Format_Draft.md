## Candidate Title

Interferon remodeling persists despite uncertain B cell state assignments in systemic lupus erythematosus

## Candidate Abstract

Systemic lupus erythematosus alters B cell abundance and transcription, but inferred effects can depend on cell state definitions. We reanalysed public single-cell data using disease-blind reconstruction and donor-aware inference. Among 150,402 discovery B-lineage cells, end-to-end resampling failed a prespecified antibody-secreting-cell overlap criterion. Propagating assignment uncertainty preserved the primary non-significant abundance result and conventional-B interferon effects. The interferon program replicated in 43 independent childhood donors using source-label-defined B cells despite weak genome-wide agreement. A separate label-agnostic mapping attempt failed reference calibration after normalization correction, precluding corrected disease estimates. STAT1/STAT2 analyses supplied convergent observational evidence, although broader interferon-gene depletion attenuated discovery STAT2. These findings support within-compartment interferon remodeling without establishing a stable taxonomy, causal regulator or unique upstream stimulus.
