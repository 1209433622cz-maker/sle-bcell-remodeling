## Operational decisions

1. Initial submission does not require special formatting. PDF and Word are accepted, and a combined reviewer-facing manuscript is encouraged. The current separate high-resolution figure files remain suitable portal assets.
2. The Reporting Summary is requested with a revised manuscript after peer review. The journal encourages earlier completion, but it is not an initial-submission blocker unless the live portal specifically requires it.
3. The HTML submission guide still contains wording that an Editorial Policy Checklist is needed before peer review. Its linked official PDF, however, states that the form is no longer required and has been removed. Therefore the retired one-page PDF must not be uploaded or represented as a completed form.
4. Policy compliance remains mandatory. The repository Markdown checklist is an internal audit only. Current portal questions must be answered, and any new portal- or editor-supplied form must be frozen and completed if requested.
5. Supplementary Information is correctly supplied as one merged PDF. It contains no Supplementary Methods, and the journal does not edit or typeset it.
6. Current figure engineering is aligned with the published guidance: white background, Arial-compatible sans serif, 8 pt visible text, at least 1 pt positive line width, colour-safe contrast, and single-file multipart figures.
7. The journal scope explicitly includes computational modelling, disease applications, multi-omics integration and single-cell systems biology. The manuscript's layered identity-composition-transcription design is in scope, provided the central contribution remains the systems-level reproducibility hierarchy rather than a universal B-cell taxonomy.
8. The current original-research APC is GBP 2,690 / USD 3,490 / EUR 2,990, subject to applicable taxes. The price is determined at acceptance. Institutional coverage, waiver eligibility and author payment authorization remain unresolved and are not inferred from the public price page.
