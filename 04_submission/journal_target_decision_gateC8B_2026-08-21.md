# Gate C8B journal target decision

**Decision date:** 20 August 2026

## Frozen route

1. **Primary submission: Genome Medicine.** Best alignment with the actual human disease-genomics and immunogenomics evidence stack. The official scope includes genomics and multi-omics applications to understand disease, including immunogenomics and systems medicine. The main editorial risk is broad-interest novelty in a public-data-only study.
2. **First transfer-ready backup: Communications Biology.** Appropriate for a technically sound, well-supported biological advance important to a specialist community. Reformatting is manageable from the five-figure package.
3. **Disease-specialist backup: Journal of Autoimmunity.** Strong topical fit, but likely to press harder for direct functional mechanism.
4. **Reach only after new functional evidence: Nature Communications.** The present package is rigorous, but without matched patient perturbation, direct binding or prospective validation, the broad conceptual-advance threshold makes this an inefficient first submission.

## Gate C8B evidence update

Gate C8B adds seven reviewer-facing supplementary figures, corrected Figure 5 source-data labels, a unified statistical/multiplicity section and a deterministic complete-results archive with 12 full gene-level branches and 12 sanitized design matrices. The scientific evidence ceiling is unchanged: matched patient perturbation, direct binding and prospective clinical validation are still absent. Genome Medicine remains the best-fit first submission; a higher-risk upper-Q1 route would require new experimental or prospective evidence rather than more retrospective polishing.

## Genome Medicine requirements frozen into Gate C8B

- Research article with significant new findings and broad interest.
- Structured Background/Methods/Results/Conclusions abstract, maximum 350 words.
- Three to ten keywords.
- Background, Methods, Results, Discussion and Conclusions.
- Full Declarations section, including ethics, consent, data, competing interests, funding, contributions and acknowledgements.
- Double-line spacing, line numbering and page numbering.
- Figure titles no longer than 15 words, legends no longer than 300 words and individual figure files below 10 MB.
- Editable manuscript and machine-readable supporting data.
