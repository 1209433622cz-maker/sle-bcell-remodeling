# Figure 4 legend draft: independent GSE135779 IFN/ISG validation

**Figure 4 | Independent GSE135779 analysis replicates the frozen IFN/ISG program within a broad conventional-B compartment.**

**a,** Forest plot of SLE-minus-control standardized IFN/ISG program-score
differences in the prespecified childhood analysis (11 controls and 32 SLE donors),
combined childhood-adult analysis (16 controls and 38 SLE donors), secondary adult
analysis (5 controls and 6 SLE donors), and combined analyses requiring at least 20
or 100 mapped cells per donor. Points show effect estimates and horizontal lines show
95% confidence intervals from linear models with HC3 sandwich uncertainty. Reported
q values use Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment across the four frozen confirmatory
programs within each analysis.

**b,** Cross-dataset comparison of the frozen IFN/ISG program in GSE174188 primary,
GSE174188 full internal validation, GSE174188 donor-nonoverlap internal validation,
GSE135779 childhood and GSE135779 combined analyses. GSE174188 validation strata are
internal to the accession; GSE135779 is the independent validation dataset.

**c,** Gene-level effects among 4,410 genes passing the respective expression filters
in both the GSE174188 primary and GSE135779 childhood analyses. Frozen IFN-arm genes
are highlighted and representative genes are labelled. Spearman rho=0.026 describes
the complete shared tested-gene set. Ten IFN genes were jointly tested and all ten
had positive effects in both datasets. The panel therefore demonstrates
program-specific coherence against low genome-wide agreement.

**d,** Influence analysis for the GSE135779 childhood IFN/ISG estimate. Donor
leave-one-out estimates are shown for all 43 deletions, and source-label sensitivity
estimates are shown after separately omitting each of B-caSC0 to B-caSC7 without
reselecting donors. The full childhood estimate is 1.042; donor-deletion estimates
range from 0.987 to 1.094 and source-label omission estimates from 1.019 to 1.051.

GSE135779 effects use donors as biological replicates. The source mapping authorizes
a broad conventional-B analog, not a hard naive/memory subtype. The adult estimate
is directional and underpowered. Association rather than causation is the binding
interpretation.
